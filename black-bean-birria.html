Black Bean Birria

Serves 3 · 45m

vegetarianbeanscheeseone-potdinner

A meat-free version of the Mexican stew, with black beans slowly simmered in ancho chilli, cumin and cinnamon.

Ingredients

To serve

Method

  1. Soak the 1 dried ancho chilli in hot water to soften.
  2. Heat the 2 tbsp neutral oil in a large pan over a medium heat.
  3. Add the 1 onion and 1 celery stick and cook for 8-10 minutes, until softened.
  4. Stir in the 4 garlic cloves, the ½ red chilli and the 1 tsp cumin seeds or 2 tsp ground cumin, along with the 2 tsp smoked paprika and ¼ tsp chilli powder if you are not using the ancho chilli.
  5. Cook for 2 minutes, until fragrant.
  6. If you are serving with rice, get it on to cook now.
  7. Lift the ancho chilli out of the water, roughly chop it and add it to the pan with the 2 tbsp tomato purée and ½ tsp ground cinnamon.
  8. Cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring frequently, until the purée darkens. Add a splash of water if it starts to stick.
  9. Pour in the 700g jar of black beans with their liquid and the 200ml vegetable stock.
  10. Bring to a simmer, cover and cook for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the stew is chocolate brown and about the consistency of a thick soup.
  11. Warm the rotis, flatbreads or tortillas in the microwave or in a dry pan, just until lightly browned.
  12. Check the stew for seasoning and add the juice of ½ a lime for freshness.
  13. Cut the remaining lime into wedges.
  14. Ladle the stew into bowls and top with the 2 sliced avocados, the 200g crumbled feta, the coriander and a pinch of chilli flakes.
  15. Serve with the lime wedges and the warm bread or rice, plus any other toppings you fancy.

Notes

  • The ancho chilli is optional, but worth seeking out — it gives a real depth of smoky heat. Without it, use the smoked paprika and chilli powder.
  • Hearty enough as a stew on its own; rice, crunchy tacos or soft tortillas make it more filling.
