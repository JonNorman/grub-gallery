Bubbling One-Pan Cheesy Tomato Butter Beans

Serves 2 · 30m

vegetarianbeansone-potlunchdinner

Fresh tomatoes cooked down with butter beans and melted mozzarella and cheddar, all in one pan.

Ingredients

To serve

Method

  1. Heat the 3 tbsp olive oil in a large, wide pan over a medium-low heat.
  2. Cut the tops off the 6 tomatoes and nestle them cut-side down around the edge of the pan.
  3. Add the 1 small onion to the middle of the pan and stir gently so it is coated in the oil.
  4. Cover and cook for 12-15 minutes, until the tomatoes have softened and the onion has turned translucent, adding a splash of boiling water if anything starts to catch.
  5. Peel away the tomato skins and discard them.
  6. Crush the tomatoes into the pan with the back of a fork, keeping some texture rather than working them smooth.
  7. Add the 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp tomato purée, 1 tsp dried oregano and 1 tsp dried rosemary with a good pinch of salt and pepper.
  8. Stir well and simmer for a few minutes, until fragrant.
  9. Stir through most of the 10g basil or parsley, saving some to garnish.
  10. Pour in the 660g jar of butter beans with their bean stock, stir to combine and cook for 2 minutes until warmed through.
  11. Tear in the 75g mozzarella and add the 75g cheddar, stirring until they melt into the sauce and it turns rich and creamy.
  12. Serve hot, scattered with the remaining basil or parsley, with crusty bread for dunking and the dressed salad leaves alongside.

Notes

Peeling the tomato skins is worth the effort: the juices break down into something silky rather than stringy.
