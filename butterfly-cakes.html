Butterfly Cakes

Makes 12 · 40m · plus 30m waiting

vegetariansnack

Before you start heat the oven to 180°C fan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cream the 85g butter and 85g sugar together.
  2. Beat in the 1 egg.
  3. Sift the 120g flour and the 1 small tsp baking powder together and fold into the creamed mixture.
  4. Divide the mixture evenly into small paper cases.
  5. Bake at 180°C fan for 18-20 mins.
  6. Form butterflies with the butter cream.
