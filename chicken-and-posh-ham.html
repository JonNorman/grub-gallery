Chicken and Posh Ham

To prepare the chicken

  1. Grate the 35g Parmesan and pick the leaves off the stalks of the 2 sprigs of thyme.
  2. Carefully score the underside of the 2 chicken breasts in a criss-cross fashion with a small knife. Season with a little freshly ground black pepper (you don't need salt as the prosciutto is quite salty).
  3. Lay your breasts next to each other and sprinkle over most of the thyme leaves. Grate a little zest from the 1 lemon over them and sprinkle with Parmesan.
  4. Lay 3 prosciutto slices on each chicken breast overlapping them slightly. Drizzle a little olive oil and sprinkle with the remaining thyme leaves.
  5. Put a square of plastic wrap over each breast and give them a few really good bashes with the bottom of a saucepan until they are about 1cm thick.

To cook the chicken

  1. Put a frying pan on a medium heat. Remove the plastic wrap and carefully transfer the chicken breasts, prosciutto side down, into the pan. Drizzle over some olive oil. Cook for 3 minutes on each side, turning halfway through giving the ham side an extra 30 seconds to crisp up.

To serve

  1. Either serve the chicken breasts whole or cut them into thick slices and pile them on a plate. Serve with lemon wedges for squeezing over and a good drizzle of olive oil.

Notes

Lovely with mashed potatoes and green veggies or a crunchy salad.
