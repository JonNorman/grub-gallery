Chickpea Caesar Salad

Serves 4 · 30m

pescatarianfishchickpeaslunchdinner

A Caesar salad with chickpeas in place of the chicken, with baked ciabatta croutons and a creamy anchovy dressing.

Before you start heat the oven to 180°C.

Ingredients

Dressing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Tear the 150g ciabatta into chunky croutons.
  3. Tumble the croutons onto a baking tray with the 2 tbsp olive oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  4. Bake for 8-10 minutes, turning often so they crisp evenly, until golden.
  5. Meanwhile, add the 2 anchovies, 1 grated garlic clove, 4 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 20g Parmesan and the juice of 1 lemon to an empty jar or mixing bowl.
  6. Shake or mix well to combine, then check the seasoning. It should be the consistency of yoghurt — add a splash of water to loosen it if needed.
  7. Tip the dressing into a large mixing bowl and add the 1 cos lettuce, the drained 660g jar of chickpeas and half the croutons.
  8. Toss well to coat.
  9. Tip onto a serving platter, top with the remaining croutons and shave over extra Parmesan.

Notes

This dressing is the shortcut version, built on mayonnaise; a from-scratch Caesar dressing works just as well.
