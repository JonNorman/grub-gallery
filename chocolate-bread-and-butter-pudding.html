Chocolate Bread and Butter Pudding

vegetarianbreaddessert

Before you start heat the oven to 180°C. Needs 2 hours standing at room temperature, then 48 hours in the fridge before cooking.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Remove the crusts from the 9 slices of white bread and cut each slice into 4 triangles.
  2. Place the 150g dark chocolate, 450ml whipping cream, 5 tbsp dark rum, 120g caster sugar, 85g butter, and pinch of cinnamon into a bain-marie. Wait until everything has melted and give it a good stir.
  3. In a separate bowl, whisk the 3 large eggs and then pour over the chocolate mixture, thoroughly blending them together with a whisk.
  4. Spoon a 1cm layer of the chocolate mixture into the base of the dish.
  5. Arrange half the triangles over the chocolate layer in overlapping rows.
  6. Pour half the remaining chocolate mixture over the bread as evenly as possible.
  7. Repeat the last two steps and press the bread down gently so that it gets covered evenly with the liquid as it cools.
  8. Cover the dish with clingfilm and allow to stand at room temperature for 2 hours before putting in fridge for 48 hours before cooking.
  9. Preheat oven to 180°C, remove clingfilm, and bake for 30-35 mins.
  10. Let it stand for 10 mins and serve with double cream or ice cream.
