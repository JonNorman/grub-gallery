Chocolate Chip Brownies

Makes 16 · 45m · plus 1-2h waiting

vegetariansnackdessert

Before you start heat the oven to 160°C fan and grease a 28 x 18cm tin. They have to cool completely in the tin before they can be cut.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 160°C fan and grease a 28 x 18cm tin.
  2. Melt the 100g butter or margarine and the 50g dark chocolate together over hot water, then take off the heat and leave to cool.
  3. Stir in the 175g dark soft brown sugar.
  4. Mix the 2 beaten eggs with the ½ tsp vanilla extract, then add to the mixture.
  5. Mix in the 100g self-raising flour, a pinch of salt and the 50g chocolate chips.
  6. Pour into the tin and bake at 160°C fan for 25-30 minutes, until the top is crispy and the inside still soft.
  7. Leave in the tin until completely cold before cutting into squares.

Notes

The original uses 50g chopped walnuts where this has chocolate chips.
