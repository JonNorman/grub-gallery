Chocolate Snowballs

20m · plus 1h waiting

vegetariansnack

Before you start needs 1 hour setting in the fridge.

Ingredients

Method

  1. In a large bowl, mix together the 500g crushed digestive biscuits and 6 tbsp cocoa powder.
  2. Melt the 100g butter in a pan, then pour into the cocoa mixture.
  3. Gradually add the 400g condensed milk, whilst stirring, to get a sticky but not sloppy consistency.
  4. Roll the mixture into small balls, dip them in the 6 tbsp icing sugar and place each into a petit four case.
  5. Leave to set in the fridge for 1 hour.
