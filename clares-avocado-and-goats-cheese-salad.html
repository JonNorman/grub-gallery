Clare's Avocado and Goat's Cheese Salad

10m

vegetariancheeselunch

Ingredients

Method

  1. Combine the salad leaves, sliced avocado and beetroot, then crumble over the goat's cheese.
  2. Dress with the aged balsamic vinegar and extra virgin olive oil, and season with black pepper.

Notes

  • You could add: peppers, walnuts, pears, and use other cheeses such as gorgonzola or feta.
  • The original document gave no quantities and no method — the steps above are the plain assembly, so adjust freely. It also contained Harissa Chickpeas, now a separate note.
