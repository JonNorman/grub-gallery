Clare's Chicken and Ham Pie

chickenpastrydinner

Before you start heat the oven to 200°C fan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Chop the 150g-200g button mushrooms into quarters and the 2 large carrots into chunks (thick rings then chopped into quarters/sixths).
  2. Trim crappy bits off the 4 or 5 chicken breasts and cut into chunks.
  3. Melt the 25g butter on low/medium heat (almost all the way round to simmer setting).
  4. Add the chicken. Keep turning until white all over and then a bit longer (roughly 5 mins) until largest chunk is white inside.
  5. Tip the chicken into a bowl so the pan can be drained then put the chicken back in.
  6. Tear the 225g wafer thin ham into chunks and add to the pan.
  7. Turn the heat back on and stir until all mixed up and the ham is looking slightly cooked (about 4 to 5 mins).
  8. Add the carrots and mushrooms and stir continuously until the mushrooms look cooked (about 3 to 5 minutes).
  9. Add the 300-400ml white wine and stir in, leave to simmer for about 10 minutes, then stir it and simmer for about another 5 minutes (until liquid reduced by about half).
  10. While simmering, make 150ml of chicken stock in a jug and mix in the 410g evaporated milk.
  11. Put the 25g plain flour in a bowl. Add a small bit of the stock/milk mix and stir (with beater) to form a thick paste, then add a bit more and stir to make a thin paste, then add the rest and stir (this is to ensure flour evenly distributed without lumps).
  12. Once the reduction is complete, pour the mix into the pan and stir thoroughly.
  13. Allow to come to the boil and stir while it thickens (only takes a few minutes).
  14. Add black pepper during thickening.
  15. Turn off heat once thickened (not too thick). Can leave like this if making pie later.
  16. Preheat oven to 200°C fan.
  17. Use the butter to lightly grease the dish (including the rim).
  18. Pour the pan contents into the dish and cover with the puff pastry and use roller to trim and then glaze with the 1 tsp milk (don't make any holes).
  19. Cook for 25 minutes, doing the chips when appropriate.
