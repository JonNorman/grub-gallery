Classic Jacket Potatoes

Serves 4 · 1h25m

potatocheeselunchdinner

Jacket potatoes with crisp skins and soft middles, filled with sour cream and a choice of toppings.

Before you start heat the oven to 200°C fan.

Ingredients

Potatoes

Toppings (any or all)

Method

  1. Rub the 2 tsp olive oil over the 4 large baking potatoes.
  2. Put them on the top shelf of the oven and bake at 200°C fan for 20 minutes.
  3. Turn the oven down to 170°C fan and bake for another 45 minutes-1 hour, until the skin is crisp and the flesh soft.
  4. Cut a cross in the top of each potato and squeeze the sides to open it up.
  5. Spoon in the 200ml sour cream and add your choice of toppings: cheddar, coleslaw, baked beans, or tuna mixed with sweetcorn and mayonnaise.
