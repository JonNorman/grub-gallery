Courgette Tuna Pasta

Serves 2 · 20m

pescatarianfishpastadinner

A quick storecupboard pasta — courgettes fried down with lemon zest, then tuna, olives and basil stirred through.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Fry the 2 courgettes in olive oil over a medium heat with the zest of 1 lemon and a pinch of salt, until softened and lightly golden.
  2. Cook the 200g farfalle in well-salted boiling water until al dente.
  3. Drain the pasta and combine with the courgette.
  4. Add the 1 tin of tuna to the courgette and pasta.
  5. Throw in the Kalamata olives and the basil.

Notes

Typed up from memory rather than a source, so the pasta weight and the yield are a reconstruction — the original recorded neither.
