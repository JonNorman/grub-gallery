Creamy Boursin & Courgette Gnocchi

Serves 4 · 20m

vegetariangnocchicheesedinner

Boursin melts into a cheat's creamy sauce for gnocchi, courgettes and peas.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the 2 tbsp olive oil in your largest frying or sauté pan over a medium-high heat.
  2. Fry the 500g courgettes with a pinch of salt for 8-9 minutes, until soft and turning golden.
  3. Meanwhile, bring a pan of salted water to the boil.
  4. A few minutes before the courgettes are ready, add the 500g gnocchi and 200g frozen peas to the water and simmer for 3 minutes.
  5. Scoop out a mugful of the cooking water, then drain the gnocchi and peas.
  6. Add the zest and juice of ½ unwaxed lemon to the courgettes, then tip in the drained gnocchi and peas and stir together.
  7. Add the 150g Boursin Garlic & Herbs and a glug of the cooking water, and stir over the heat until the cheese has melted and everything is coated in a glossy sauce. Add more cooking water if needed.
  8. Stir in most of the shredded basil, then scoop onto plates.
  9. Scatter over the remaining basil and a pinch of chilli flakes to serve.

Notes

Sweat some leeks with the courgettes and add a tin of butter beans at step 6 to boost the fibre and make the dish go further.
