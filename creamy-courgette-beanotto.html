Creamy Courgette Beanotto

Serves 2-3 · 30m

vegetarianbeanscheeseone-potdinner

White beans cooked risotto-style with courgette, lemon, crème fraîche and Parmesan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Squeeze as much liquid as you can out of the 1 grated courgette.
  2. Toast the handful of pine nuts in a dry pan for 2-3 minutes until golden, then set aside.
  3. Heat the 2 tbsp olive oil in a large pan over a medium heat.
  4. Add the 1 sliced courgette with a pinch of salt and cook for 7-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until starting to brown.
  5. Add the grated courgette and the 2 garlic cloves and cook for a further 2-3 minutes, until the courgette softens.
  6. Grate in the zest of ½ lemon.
  7. Pour in the 570g jar of white beans with their liquid.
  8. Fill the empty jar a quarter full with water, swirl it around and add that to the pan too — it acts as a stock. Stir to combine.
  9. Season well with cracked black pepper, tasting before you add any salt as the beans are already well seasoned.
  10. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the 1 heaped tbsp crème fraîche and the 45g Parmesan, working fairly quickly so the crème fraîche does not split.
  11. Stir in the 15g parsley and tear in most of the basil, saving a few to garnish.
  12. Add the juice of ½ lemon and check the seasoning.
  13. Spoon into bowls and grate over extra lemon zest and extra Parmesan.
  14. Scatter over the toasted pine nuts and the remaining basil, and finish with a drizzle of olive oil.

Notes

  • Something crunchy on top makes a real difference — pine nuts, toasted seeds or breadcrumbs all work.
  • Make it vegan: use a plant-based crème fraîche and a vegan alternative to the Parmesan.
  • The source calls for one jar of white beans and links both a 570g and a 660g jar; either size works.
  • See also Miso Mushroom Beanotto for the colder-weather version.
