Creamy Tomato and Mascarpone Radiatori with Chickpeas

Serves 3-4 · 25m

vegetarianpastachickpeascheesedinner

Radiatori and chickpeas in a white wine and passata sauce made creamy with mascarpone and Parmesan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil for the pasta.
  2. Warm the olive oil in a large frying pan over a medium-low heat and gently soften the ½ red onion until translucent.
  3. Add the 3 garlic cloves and cook for about a minute, until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the white wine, scraping up any browned bits, and let it reduce by about half.
  5. Add the 400ml passata and simmer for 5-10 minutes, until the sauce is as thick as you like it.
  6. Meanwhile, cook the 300g radiatori until a couple of minutes short of al dente.
  7. Save a mugful of the pasta water, then drain.
  8. Stir the 125g mascarpone into the sauce until smooth.
  9. Mix in the basil and 45g Parmesan, and season with salt to taste.
  10. Add the radiatori and the 400g tin of chickpeas to the sauce and cook together for a minute or two, adding pasta water a splash at a time until it is the consistency you like.
  11. Serve topped with extra Parmesan.
