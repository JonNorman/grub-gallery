Crispy Kale and Bulgar Wheat Salad

Serves 2 · 30m

vegetarianbulgurlunch

Before you start heat the oven to 200°C.

Ingredients

To serve

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
  2. Mix the 200g bulgur wheat, 5cm ginger, zest of 2 lemons, sea salt, and 400ml boiling water in a roasting tin.
  3. Toss the 200g kale with the olive oil and tip evenly over the bulgur wheat.
  4. Transfer to the oven and roast for 15 minutes.
  5. Whisk together the 1 heaped tbsp za'atar, 1 tbsp lemon juice, and 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil and set aside.
  6. Mix the cooked bulgur wheat and the crispy kale with the dressing.
  7. Scatter over the 100g goats cheese, 50g almonds, pomegranate seeds, and coriander.
  8. Serve hot with the yoghurt alongside.
