Fusilli a la Vodka with Basil and Parmesan

25m

vegetarianpastacheesedinner

Ingredients

Method

Stops your screen dimming and locking while your hands are busy. It switches off when you leave this page.

  1. Heat olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the ½ shallot and 1 garlic clove and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the 7 tbsp tomato purée and cook, stirring occasionally, until the purée is brick red and starts to caramelise, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the 2 tbsp vodka and cook, stirring constantly, until liquid is mostly evaporated, about 2 minutes.
  4. Add the 230g double cream and 1 tsp chilli flakes and stir until well blended. Season with salt and pepper; remove from heat.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the 450g fusilli in a large pot of boiling salted water, stirring occasionally, until al dente. Drain, reserving 2 or 3 ladles of pasta cooking liquid.
  6. Add the pasta to the pan with the sauce along with the butter and 1 or 2 ladles of pasta cooking liquid. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly and adding more pasta cooking liquid if needed, until butter has melted and a thick, glossy sauce has formed, about 2 minutes.
  7. Season with salt and pepper and add Parmesan, tossing to coat. Divide pasta among bowls, then top with basil and more Parmesan.
