Fusilli a la Vodka with Basil and Parmesan

vegetarianpastadinner

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the ½ shallot and 1 garlic clove and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the 7 tbsp tomato paste and cook, stirring occasionally, until paste is brick red and starts to caramelise, about 5 minutes.
  3. Add the 2 tbsp vodka and cook, stirring constantly, until liquid is mostly evaporated, about 2 minutes.
  4. Add the 230g double cream and 1 tsp red pepper flakes and stir until well blended. Season with salt and pepper; remove from heat.
  5. Meanwhile, cook the 450g fusilli in a large pot of boiling salted water, stirring occasionally, until al dente. Drain, reserving 2 or 3 ladles of pasta cooking liquid.
  6. Add the pasta to skillet with sauce along with the butter and 1 or 2 ladles of pasta cooking liquid. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly and adding more pasta cooking liquid if needed, until butter has melted and a thick, glossy sauce has formed, about 2 minutes.
  7. Season with salt and pepper and add Parmesan, tossing to coat. Divide pasta among bowls, then top with basil and more Parmesan.
