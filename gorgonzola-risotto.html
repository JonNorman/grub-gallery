Gorgonzola Risotto

Serves 3 · 30m

vegetarianriceone-potdinner

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the 1 tbsp of olive oil and ½ (12.5g) of the butter in a wide, shallow pan. Add the 2 leeks and 1 garlic clove and cook over a gentle heat for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Add the 125g risotto rice and stir to coat in the oil.
  3. Increase the heat a little and pour in the 1 glass of white wine. Simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Add a ladleful of the 900ml vegetable stock and simmer, stirring occasionally until all the liquid has been absorbed. Continue adding the stock in this way until it has all been used and the rice is tender and creamy.
  5. Season and stir in the 2 tbsp tarragon. Remove from the heat and fold the remaining butter and the 125g gorgonzola cheese through the risotto. Serve immediately.
