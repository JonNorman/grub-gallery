Harissa Chickpeas

10m

veganchickpeaslunch

Ingredients

Method

  1. Grind the ½ tsp cumin seeds and ½ tsp fennel seeds together, but not to a powder.
  2. Drain the 1 large tin of chickpeas.
  3. Throw the spices over the chickpeas with lemon juice.
  4. Add harissa paste and a pinch of salt.

Notes

Separated out from Clare's Avocado and Goat's Cheese Salad, where this was recorded in the same document. The two may have been intended to go together.
