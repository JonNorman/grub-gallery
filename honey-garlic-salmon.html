Honey Garlic Salmon

Serves 4 · 35m · plus 15m-4h waiting

pescatarianfishricedinner

Salmon marinated in honey, soy, garlic and ginger, baked and finished with the reduced

Before you start needs 15 minutes to 4 hours marinating.

Ingredients

Salmon

To serve

Method

To marinate

  1. Whisk the 80ml soy sauce, 115g clear honey, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 3 garlic cloves and 1 tsp ginger root together in a bowl.
  2. Put the 4 salmon fillets in a shallow dish or food bag and pour over half the marinade, keeping the rest back for the glaze. Turn to coat and refrigerate for 15 minutes to 4 hours.

To cook

  1. Heat the oven to 170°C fan and line a baking tray with foil or baking paper.
  2. Lay the salmon skin-side down on the tray and bake at 170°C fan for 15–20 minutes, about 10 minutes per inch of thickness, until the thickest part reaches 63°C. Grill for the last minute for crisper edges.
  3. Meanwhile, cook the 300g basmati rice according to the packet.
  4. Pour the reserved marinade into a small saucepan, bring to the boil, then simmer for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened. Watch it closely — it bubbles up and burns quickly.
  5. Steam the broccoli for 4–5 minutes, adding the 4 pak choi for the last 2–3 minutes, until both are just tender.

To serve

  1. Spoon the glaze over the salmon and scatter with the spring onions and sesame seeds. Serve with the rice, broccoli and pak choi.

Notes

  • The rice, broccoli and pak choi are our addition; the original suggests steamed broccoli and rice.
  • If you add the used marinade to the glaze as well, boil it hard for several minutes.
  • Marinating can stretch to 8 hours at a push. Frozen salmon works if fully thawed.
  • Boneless chicken breasts or thighs work instead: marinate for about an hour and bake until cooked through.
  • Leftovers keep in the fridge for a few days.
