Honey Mustard Chicken Pasta

Serves 3

chickenpastalunch

Ingredients

Method

  1. Boil the 300g farfalle, then cool under running water.
  2. Mix the 3 tbsp mayo, 1 heaped tsp wholegrain mustard and 1 tsp clear honey in a large bowl and loosen with a little water to make a dressing with the consistency of double cream.
  3. Add the pasta, 300g cooked chicken, 4 spring onions, basil and 4 tomatoes, season to taste, then gently mix together.
