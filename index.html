      About Grub Gallery

      A collection of recipes gathered over the years — family recipes, cookbook favourites and things worth keeping from the internet. They live as plain notes but are published here so that they can be shared with others and used to drive the weekly shop.

      Planning a week

      Press + on a card to drop it into the next free day, or drag a card onto a particular day. The week starts on Saturday by default but this can be changed by updating Starts.

      Skipping a day

      Tap an empty day in the week plan to mark it Skipped — this is useful when eating out, using leftovers, going away etc. Tap again to free it. This day will then be skipped when adding recipes to the plan.

      The shopping list

      Shopping list gathers every ingredient across the planned meals and groups them, so three recipes wanting Parmesan appear together with each amount. Quantities are listed rather than summed up — you can take a view as to how much of everything you need. Salt, pepper, oil and water are left out.

      Tick items off as you shop and they drop into Completed at the bottom, so what is left stays short. Print gives a paper copy, and Copy puts it on the clipboard as plain text for a message or a notes app.

      Sending a week plan summary

      Print view Provides an image of the week plan that can then be saved / shared via the usual channels or printed off to stick on the fridge.

      Sharing a week plan

      The address bar always holds the current plan, so there is nothing to press: copy the URL and send it to whoever is cooking or doing the shop. Opening someone else's link simply shows their week, and editing it makes it yours.

      Finding something to cook

      Type what you have into the search box and pick from the suggestions. Terms narrow together, so chickpeas feta shows only recipes needing both. The tag buttons below filter by things like meal, diet, wait times and baby suitability.

      Keyboard

      Press ⌘K to jump to the search box from anywhere.

      ⌘/ opens this help page and closes it again.

      Esc closes whatever is open on the page.

      Tooltips and keyboard shortcuts are shown on the buttons themselves if you hover over.
