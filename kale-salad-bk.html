Kale Salad (BK)

Serves 6-8

vegetarianlunch

Before you start needs 30 minutes resting at room temperature, or up to one day in the refrigerator.

Ingredients

Dressing

Salad

Method

  1. Mix together the 125ml olive oil, juice of 3 lemons, 3 garlic cloves and 1 tsp chilli flakes in a large bowl.
  2. After washing and drying the 500g kale, cut and discard the tough inner ribs. Chop the leaves into smaller pieces and add to the bowl. Mix with the dressing so every piece is coated, using salad servers or clean hands. Leave to rest for 30 minutes at room temperature, or up to one day in the refrigerator.
  3. Before serving, toss the kale again and add the 150g Parmesan, 4 tbsp breadcrumbs and flaky sea salt and cracked pepper. Toss again and taste, adding more salt, pepper, Parmesan or breadcrumbs as needed. Serve immediately.

Notes

Serves 6-8 as a side.
