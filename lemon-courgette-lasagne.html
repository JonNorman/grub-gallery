Lemon Courgette Lasagne

1h15m

vegetarianpastacheesedinner

A creamy courgette lasagne layered with lemon béchamel and pesto.

Before you start heat the oven to 180°C fan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan.
  2. Thinly slice the 4 courgettes.
  3. Fry the 2-3 garlic cloves, chopped (or 2 tsp jarred garlic) in oil, then add the courgettes. Fry until very soft. Add thyme and season to taste with salt and black pepper.
  4. Make a béchamel with the butter, plain flour and milk, then stir in nutmeg, salt and pepper, the zest of 1 lemon and the juice of ½ lemon.
  5. Assemble the lasagne in layers: courgettes, blobs of béchamel, blobs of pesto, then a lasagne sheet. Repeat 2-3 times, depending on how much filling you have.
  6. Top with mozzarella, Parmesan and black pepper.
  7. Bake at 180°C fan for 30-35 minutes, until bubbling and golden.
