Miso Mushroom Beanotto

Serves 2 · 35m

vegetarianbeansone-potdinner

White beans cooked risotto-style in a miso and mushroom sauce, finished with Parmesan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Melt the 30g butter in a large pan over a medium heat.
  2. Fry the 250-300g mushrooms for roughly 5 minutes, until browned.
  3. Add the 570g jar of white beans with their bean stock.
  4. Fill the empty jar a quarter full with water, swirl it around and add that to the pan too.
  5. Stir everything together and cook for 4-5 minutes, until the beans are warmed through.
  6. Stir in the 1 heaped tbsp miso and cook for a further minute, until the sauce is rich and glossy. Add a splash of water to loosen it if necessary.
  7. Just before serving, stir through the 45g grated Parmesan and a few cracks of black pepper.
  8. Spoon into bowls and finish with extra Parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil.
  9. Serve with crusty bread, if you like.

Notes

The source suggests leaving out the Parmesan for a vegan version; the butter would need swapping for a plant-based alternative too.
