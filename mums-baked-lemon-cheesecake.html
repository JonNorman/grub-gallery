Mum's Baked Lemon Cheesecake

1h20m · plus 3h waiting

vegetariancheesedessert

Before you start heat the oven to 160°C fan. Needs 1hr chilling for the base, then at least 2hr or overnight chilling once baked.

Ingredients

Method

  1. For the base, put the 250g packet digestives in a food processor and whiz until fine. Melt the 125g unsalted butter, add to the biscuits and mix in the processor until well combined.
  2. Tip the mixture into a greased 20.5cm springform tin. Using the back of a spoon, press evenly into the base. Chill for 1hr until firm.
  3. Preheat the oven to 160°C fan. Grate the rind from the 1 large unwaxed lemon and put aside. Halve the lemon, cut 3 thin slices from one half and put to one side. Squeeze the juice from the lemon into a small bowl or cup.
  4. To make the filling, put the lemon rind, lemon juice, 2 x 250g tubs Quark, 142ml sour cream, 2 medium eggs, 175g golden caster sugar, 1½ tsp vanilla extract and 1 level tbsp cornflour into a large bowl. Using an electric mixer, whisk together until thick and smooth.
  5. Pour mixture into tin and shake gently to level. Bake for 30 min. Put the lemon slices, overlapping on top. Bake for another 20-25 mins until just set and golden brown. Turn off oven, leaving the cheesecake inside and door ajar. When cheesecake is cool, chill for at least 2hr or overnight.
