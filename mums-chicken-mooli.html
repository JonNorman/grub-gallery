Mum's Chicken Mooli

40m

chickenone-potdinner

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pan, add the 4 chicken breasts and quickly fry all over. Remove the chicken with a slotted spoon.
  2. Add more oil to the pan and fry the 6 cardamom, 6 cloves and 5cm cinnamon stick for 1 min.
  3. Add the 1 large onion and fry until soft.
  4. Place the 2 garlic cloves, 3.5cm piece of ginger, 3 green chillies and juice of 1 lemon in a food processor and work to a smooth paste.
  5. Add the paste to the pan with the 1 tsp turmeric and cook for 5 mins.
  6. Melt the 50g creamed coconut in the 150ml hot water and add to the pan and season.
  7. Simmer for 2 mins, then add the chicken and any juices.
  8. Simmer for 15-20 mins until tender.
