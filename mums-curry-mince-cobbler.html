Mum's Curry Mince Cobbler

beefdinner

Before you start heat the oven to 200°C.

Ingredients

Method

Prepare the meat

  1. Heat oil in a pan and fry the 1 onion, 500g minced beef and 100g mushrooms.
  2. Stir in the 25g flour and cook for 1 min.
  3. Remove from the heat and stir in the 300ml stock.
  4. Return to the heat and bring to the boil, whilst stirring.
  5. Reduce heat and stir in the 425g can baked beans, 10-15ml curry concentrate and seasoning.
  6. Simmer for 10 mins and then turn mixture into an ovenproof casserole dish.
  7. Preheat oven to 200°C.

Prepare the cobblers

  1. Sieve the 225g self-raising flour and a pinch of salt into a bowl and rub in the 50g butter.
  2. Stir in the 150ml milk to form a soft dough.
  3. Roll out dough to 1cm thick. Using a 5cm fluted cutter, stamp out ~10 rounds.

To cook

  1. Place rounds, slightly overlapping, on top of mince mixture and bake for 20 mins.
