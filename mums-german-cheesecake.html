Mum's German Cheesecake

55m · plus 2h30m waiting

vegetariancheesedessert

Before you start heat the oven to 150°C fan. Needs 1hr chilling for the base.

Ingredients

Method

  1. For the base, put the 100g packet digestives in a food processor and whiz until fine. Melt the 50g butter, add to the biscuits and mix in the processor until well combined.
  2. Line the tin with foil, tip the mixture and using the back of a spoon, press evenly into the base. Chill for 1hr until firm.
  3. Preheat the oven to 150°C fan. Cream the 340g Quark, 85g caster sugar, 1 egg until smooth. Then add the vanilla extract and mix in.
  4. Spread the mixture on the biscuit base and bake in the centre of the oven for 30-40 mins until set.
  5. Turn off the heat and keep the cheesecake in it until cool. This helps to prevent it cracking. If liked, decorate with double cream and fruit.
