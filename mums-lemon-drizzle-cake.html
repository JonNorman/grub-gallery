Mum's Lemon Drizzle Cake

1h · plus 30m waiting

vegetariansnack

Before you start heat the oven to 180°C fan.

Ingredients

Cake

Drizzle

Method

Stops your screen dimming and locking while your hands are busy. It switches off when you leave this page.

  1. Beat all cake ingredients together and put into cake tin.
  2. Bake for 40-60 minutes at 180°C fan.
  3. Mix the 120g caster sugar and juice of 1 lemon and pour over the cooked cake (whilst still in the tin) and leave to set.
