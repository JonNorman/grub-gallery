Mum's Lemon Trifle

vegetariandessert

Before you start needs overnight chilling in the fridge.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cream the 120g butter and 180g caster sugar until pale and fluffy.
  2. Mix the 4 egg yolks and beat into mixture a little at a time with an electric mixer.
  3. Add the grated rind and juice of the 2 large lemons.
  4. Whisk the 4 egg whites and fold into creamed lemon mixture.
  5. Layer the 8 trifle sponges and mixture into a 2 part souffle dish, starting and finishing with sponge (i.e. 2 layers of cream mixture and 3 layers of sponge).
  6. Cover with foil and chill overnight in the fridge.
  7. Decorate with the 300ml whipped cream.
