Quick Salmon Parcels

Serves 2 · 35m

pescatarianfishpotatodinner

A whole dinner from one tray: salmon and green beans steamed in foil parcels with pesto, alongside buttered new potatoes.

Before you start heat the oven to 200°C fan.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan and bring a lidded pan of salted water to the boil for the 500g new potatoes.
  2. Lay a row of the green beans on a sheet of foil, large enough to fold into a parcel later. Repeat for the second parcel.
  3. Sit the 2 salmon fillets on the beans and spread the 4 tbsp green pesto along the top of each.
  4. Drizzle the 2 tbsp olive oil over the pesto with a squeeze of lemon juice, and season with black pepper.
  5. Fold the foil up into parcels, put them on a tray and bake at 200°C fan for 20 minutes. The salmon should reach 63°C.
  6. Meanwhile, boil the potatoes for 15 minutes, until soft. Drain them, add the knob of butter and a pinch of dried oregano, then shake the pan to fluff them up.

Notes

  • Swap the beans for whatever veg you like — sitting it under the salmon steams it in the parcel.
  • The parcel keeps the moisture in, so the salmon does not really dry out. 20 minutes usually does it; a thermometer is the way to be sure.
