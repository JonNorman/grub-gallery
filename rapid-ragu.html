Rapid Ragù

Serves 4 · 30m

lambcheeseone-potdinner

A lamb and lentil ragù that skips the chopping by leaning on jarred caramelised onions and garlic oil, simmered for 20 minutes and finished with grated cheddar.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Heat the 2 tbsp garlic-infused olive oil in a wide, medium saucepan with a lid, and fry the 125g pancetta cubes until they begin to crisp.
  2. Add the 500g minced lamb, breaking it up with a fork in the bacony pan as it browns.
  3. Tip in the 75g caramelised onions, 80ml Marsala, 1 x 400g tin of chopped tomatoes, 75g green lentils and 125ml water, and bring to the boil.
  4. Cover with the lid and simmer for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Sprinkle with the 50g cheddar before serving.

Notes

  • Without jarred caramelised onions, cook an onion very slowly in butter or olive oil until it caramelises; without garlic oil, grate a garlic clove or two over the cooked onions. Neither is rapid.
  • Can be made up to 3 days ahead. Cool and refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking, covered. Reheat in a saucepan over a medium heat, stirring and adding a splash of water if needed, until piping hot. Freezes for up to 3 months; thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat as above.
