Roast Pumpkin Curry

Serves 4 · 50m · 15m prep · 40m cook

Before you start heat the oven to 200°C fan.

Ingredients

For the tempering

Method

  1. First, roast the ½ pumpkin. Preheat the oven to 200°C fan. Scoop out the seeds with a spoon and discard. Cut the pumpkin into wedges around 2cm thick (leave the skin on).
  2. Place on a baking tray and toss in 1 tbsp of the coconut or vegetable oil and sprinkle with salt and ¼ tsp of the curry powder, mixing well. Roast for 20 to 25 minutes until tender and golden and starting to brown at the edges. Remove from the oven and leave to cool on the tray.
  3. To make the curry, heat a medium-sized wok or saucepan over a medium-high flame. Add the remaining 1 tbsp of oil, and, when hot, fry the 1 medium red onion until translucent.
  4. Add the 10 fresh curry leaves and 3 garlic cloves, and, about a minute later, add the 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tsp cumin seeds and 2 cardamom pods. Fry the mixture for one minute, being careful not to burn the spices, stirring and adding a little more oil if necessary.
  5. Pour in the 200ml coconut milk and add the 1½ tsp ground turmeric and remaining ¼ tsp of curry powder, 1 tsp of salt and two pieces of the roasted pumpkin for flavour. Cook so that the curry is gently bubbling for three to four minutes, then turn off the flame.
  6. Dish up by placing the roasted pumpkin wedges on a plate, and spooning the curry liquor over and around the pumpkin.
  7. Heat up the 1 tbsp oil for the tempering in a small pan and add the 10 fresh curry leaves, letting them crisp up.
  8. Scatter the tempered curry leaves over the pumpkin curry, add the 4cm ginger root and finish with squeezing over the 1 lime.
