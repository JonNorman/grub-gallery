Roast Spiced Winter Roots with Haricot Mash

Serves 4 · 1h15m

For the mash

Method

  1. Peel and crush the 2 large garlic cloves to a paste. Using a spice mill or pestle and mortar, crush the 2 tsp cumin seeds and 2 tsp coriander seeds to a coarse powder, then mix in the ½ tsp sea salt and several grinds of black pepper.
  2. Add the pinch of chilli flakes and stir in the crushed garlic and 4 tbsp groundnut oil. Set the oven at 180°C fan.
  3. Bring a couple of deep pans of water to the boil. Trim the 500g beetroot, without breaking the skin, then put them in the water and let them cook for 25-30 minutes, depending on their size, until they are soft enough to pierce easily with a skewer. Cut the 500g potatoes in half and add them to the second pan and boil them for 15 minutes.
  4. Drain the beetroot and cut them in half. If they are large, cut them into wedges. Drain the potatoes. Slice the 8 carrots in half. Toss the carrots, beetroot and potatoes in the spiced oil then transfer to a roasting tin and bake for 35-45 minutes. (I like to turn each one over halfway through cooking so they get evenly browned.)
  5. To make the mash, drain the 2 x 400g tins of beans and put them in a small pan, just covered with water. Bring to the boil, then lower the heat and leave to simmer for 10 minutes.
  6. Drain and return the beans to the empty pan, add the 2 tbsp olive oil, a good handful of parsley, a little black pepper and the 1 tbsp lemon juice, then mash to a smooth purée with a potato masher or food processor.
  7. Pile the mashed beans on a serving dish, then place the roasted spiced roots on top and serve.
