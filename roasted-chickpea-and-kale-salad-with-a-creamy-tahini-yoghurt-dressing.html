Roasted Chickpea and Kale Salad with a Creamy Tahini + Yoghurt Dressing

35m · plus 1h waiting

vegetarianchickpeascheeselunch

Before you start heat the oven to 190°C fan. Needs about 1 hour refrigerating.

Ingredients

Creamy tahini and yoghurt dressing

Roasted chickpeas

To serve

Method

Stops your screen dimming and locking while your hands are busy. It switches off when you leave this page.

  1. Place all dressing ingredients into a blender and blend until smooth and desired consistency has been achieved.
  2. Pour dressing into a large mixing bowl, top with the 1 bunch of kale and toss together until kale leaves are fully coated. Refrigerate for about 1 hour.
  3. Preheat oven to 190°C fan.
  4. Place all chickpea ingredients into another mixing bowl and toss together until completely combined. Pour mixture onto a baking sheet lined with parchment and roast for about 15 minutes. Toss mixture together with a wooden spoon and continue to roast chickpeas for about 10 minutes. Remove from heat.
  5. Pour the chickpeas over the dressed kale and finish with the ricotta salata or Parmesan. Serve.
