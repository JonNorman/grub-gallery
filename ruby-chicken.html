Chicken marinade

  1. Blitz the ingredients together in a blender to a fine paste and transfer to a bowl.
  2. Cut the 700g skinless, boneless chicken thighs into 4cm chunks. Add to marinade and turn to coat.
  3. Cover and leave to marinate for 6 - 24 hours.
  4. Heat the grill to medium-high and place the chicken on a rack in the grill pan, brush with the melted 20g unsalted butter and grill for 8-10 minutes until cooked through and nicely charred.
  5. Warm up the makhani sauce, 50ml double cream and grilled chicken and simmer very gently for 10 minutes.
  6. Serve the curry garnished with ginger root cut into matchsticks, chopped coriander and 1 tbsp pomegranate seeds, with a bowl of steamed basmati rice.

Makhani sauce

  1. Take half the 35g garlic and finely slice it. Warm a large saucepan over medium high heat and add the 175ml vegetable oil. Toss in the chopped garlic and fry until slightly crispy, and remove and place aside to drain using a slotted spoon. Remove the oil from the heat whilst you do this.
  2. Grind the remaining half of the garlic and 20g ginger root into a paste.
  3. Blitz the 880g chopped tomatoes to a fine consistency.
  4. Add the 2 bay leaves, 6 green cardamom pods, 2 black cardamom pods and 2 cinnamon sticks to the pan. Let them crackle for 1 minute, stirring gently.
  5. Turn the heat down and add the garlic & ginger paste allowing it to brown but not burn.
  6. Add the 880g chopped tomatoes, 2 tsp fine sea salt, 1½ tsp deggi mirch chilli powder to the pan. Bring to a rapid simmer and then cook until reduced by half, stirring regularly. This should take about 30 minutes.
  7. Add the 30g butter and simmer for 5 minutes.
  8. Add the 1 tsp garam masala, 20g granulated sugar, 1 tbsp clear honey, 1 tsp ground cumin, crisp garlic, 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves, and 1½ tsp dill and cook for a further 15 minutes.
  9. If using the sauce straight away then add the 80ml double cream and simmer gently for 5 minutes. If not, then allow it to cool, cover it and refrigerate it – add the cream whenever you go to eat it.
