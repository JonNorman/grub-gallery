Sesame and Miso Cookies

Makes 20 · 1h05m · plus 2-12h waiting

vegetariansnackdessert

Chewy cookies with tahini, white miso and white chocolate, rolled in sesame seeds.

Before you start needs at least 2 hours chilling, preferably overnight, before baking.

Ingredients

Method

To make the dough

  1. In a large bowl, use an electric hand mixer (or a stand mixer) to beat together the 225g unsalted butter, 200g light muscovado sugar and 100g caster sugar for 2-3 minutes until light.
  2. Add the 2 tbsp tahini, 2 tbsp white miso paste, 1 egg and 1 egg white, and mix again until smooth.
  3. Sift in the 325g plain flour, 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda, ½ tsp fine sea salt and ¼ tsp ground cinnamon, and mix until just combined.
  4. Stir in the 200g white chocolate until combined.
  5. Cover the bowl and chill the dough for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight.

To bake

  1. Heat the oven to 160°C fan and line 2 large baking sheets with baking parchment.
  2. Roll the chilled dough into even-sized balls of 55-60g each.
  3. Tip the 75g sesame seeds onto a tray or into a large shallow bowl and roll each ball in them to coat.
  4. Place 5-6 balls on each lined sheet, leaving plenty of space for them to spread. Flatten each slightly with your fingers and sprinkle with a pinch of sea salt flakes.
  5. Bake at 160°C fan for 16-18 minutes until golden.
  6. Sharply tap the underside of each sheet on the work surface to slightly deflate the cookies.
  7. Leave to cool for 5 minutes on the sheets, then transfer to a cooling rack.
  8. Repeat with the remaining dough.

Notes

  • Two sheets of 5-6 take about half the dough, so the 20 cookies bake in two batches — hence the 40 minutes of cooking.
  • Transcribed from a Waitrose magazine page (p.104); it gave 180°C, gas mark 4, written here as 160°C fan.
