Spicy Mac 'n Cheese

Serves 5 · 30m

vegetarianpastacheesedinner

Macaroni cheese cut with charred broccoli and green chillies, served with guacamole.

Ingredients

Method

  1. Salt a large pan of water, bring it to the boil and cook the 500g macaroni, usually about 10 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, make the white sauce. Melt the 100g butter in a pan, stir in the 2 tbsp plain flour to make a roux, then whisk in the 700ml milk a little at a time.
  3. Stir in the 2 tsp wholegrain mustard. Be braver with it than you would think — it is fighting the cheese for space.
  4. Add the 200g cheddar and the 200g red leicester, then turn the pan down to low to keep the sauce warm.
  5. Dry fry the 2 heads of broccoli and the 2 green chillies in a pan over a high heat, sprinkling water into the pan to help them steam. Stop when they are nicely charred and as al dente as you like.
  6. Combine the macaroni with the sauce, then stir through the broccoli and chillies.
  7. Serve with a dollop of guacamole and shredded coriander leaves.
